## Source-license notes

- Wikimedia Commons files must be handled under the license on each file page.
  The relevant verified examples above are CC BY-SA and require creator credit,
  a license link, change disclosure, and ShareAlike for adaptations.
- Pexels permits use of downloaded media in websites under the Pexels License,
  subject to its restrictions, but no file was saved when a reliable authorized
  direct download was unavailable.
- ExerciseDB/Gym Visual mirrors were excluded because public repository access
  does not grant a reusable media license.
- RepDB paid animations and preview samples were excluded.
- Flow Exercise Dataset animations were excluded because its notice describes
  them as AI-generated, unreviewed, and not professionally form-verified.
