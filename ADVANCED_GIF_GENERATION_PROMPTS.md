# Advanced Exercise GIF Generation Prompts

## Current status

No image or video generation tool is available in this environment. No GIFs
were generated, altered, or registered. These prompts are intended for an
external generator and are grounded in the exercise instructions in
`exercises.py`.

Two files already exist and must not be overwritten or registered without
source/license clearance:

- `static\exercises\157.gif` is an untracked seated animation that visually
  matches the movement pattern for exercise 152, not the standing lunge flow
  for exercise 157. Its source and license are undocumented.
- `static\exercises\176.gif` is a tracked animation that visually matches the
  thread-the-needle sequence for exercise 176. Its source and license are
  undocumented, so it is not approved for use.

Keep both existing files untouched. Do not map either file until its source
and usage rights are established; generate 157 to a separate staging filename
until its owner approves the requested `157.gif` filename.

## Shared visual requirements

Apply these requirements to every individual prompt below:

- Create an original animated exercise demonstration with one adult instructor
  and realistic, anatomically consistent proportions.
- Keep the same instructor, clothing, lighting, and plain uncluttered studio
  background across the set. Use simple opaque athletic clothing.
- Use a fixed, full-body camera with enough space around the instructor to keep
  the head, hands, feet, and any chair fully in frame for every movement.
  Compose for the website's workout-card image area without cropping limbs.
- Animate smoothly at a controlled, readable pace. Keep two complete arms and
  two complete legs attached and visible whenever their position permits;
  natural occlusion is acceptable, but no disappearing or duplicated limbs,
  warped joints, sudden pose changes, or impossible transitions.
- Follow only the listed exercise sequence. Do not add equipment or extra
  movements. Demonstrate both sides wherever the instructions require both
  sides.
- Return to the exact opening pose for a clean loop where the movement allows
  it. No labels, captions, text, logo, watermark, decorative effects, or cuts.
- Export as an animated GIF using the exact requested filename. Review the
  entire animation frame-by-frame before approving it.

## 152 - Seated Pattern Flow

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Sit tall on a stable chair with feet planted and arms at shoulder height.
2. Alternate a cross-body knee reach, an overhead reach, and a lateral toe tap.
3. Repeat the sequence smoothly without rushing or leaning from the chair.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a short, seamless, full-body demonstration of Seated Pattern Flow. Show
the adult seated upright on a stable chair, both feet initially planted and
both arms held at shoulder height. Perform the sequence in this order: alternate
a cross-body reach toward a lifted knee, return to the upright start; perform a
controlled overhead reach and return the arms to shoulder height; tap one toe
out to the side and return it, then tap the other toe and return it. Keep the
torso tall and steady throughout; do not lean to reach. Preserve the chair,
both legs, both feet, and both arms in frame. Finish seated in the exact opening
pose.

## 153 - Single-leg Balance Reach Flow

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Stand on one leg with the knee softly bent and brace your trunk.
2. Reach the free foot forward, sideways, and behind without touching down if possible.
3. Return to center after each reach and complete both sides.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a short, controlled, full-body demonstration of Single-leg Balance Reach
Flow. Start standing on the left leg with its knee softly bent and trunk braced.
Keep the right foot off the floor as it reaches forward, returns fully to
center, reaches sideways, returns to center, reaches behind, and returns to
center. Then switch support legs and repeat the same three reaches with the
left foot. Keep the standing knee aligned over its foot, hips level, and torso
quiet; use only small natural arm adjustments for balance. Do not hop or let the
free foot touch down between reaches. End balanced in the opening stance.

## 154 - Tempo Push-up with Pause

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Begin in a strong high-plank position with hands under your shoulders.
2. Lower for three seconds and pause just above the floor for one second.
3. Press up while keeping your body in one line; stop before form breaks.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a precise full-body demonstration of Tempo Push-up with Pause. Start in
a strong high plank with palms under shoulders, arms extended, legs straight,
and head, trunk, hips, and heels aligned. Lower continuously and evenly for
three seconds, keeping elbows controlled and the body in one line. Hold for one
second just above the floor without resting on it. Press smoothly back to the
same high plank. Show one clean repetition and return to the exact start pose.
Do not sag or pike the hips, flare the elbows abruptly, or lower the head alone.

## 155 - Single-leg Squat to Knee Drive

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Balance on one leg and send your hips back into a shallow single-leg squat.
2. Drive through the standing foot and bring the free knee forward.
3. Reset your balance between repetitions and train both sides equally.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a controlled, full-body demonstration of Single-leg Squat to Knee Drive.
Balance on the left foot, send the hips back into a shallow squat on that leg,
then press through the standing foot and drive the right knee forward. Pause
briefly to regain balance and return to the starting stance. Repeat on the
opposite side for the same number of repetitions. Keep the standing knee
tracking over the foot, pelvis steady, chest upright, and free leg clear of the
floor during the knee drive. No deep squat or jump.

## 157 - Complex Mobility Flow

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Step into a reverse lunge, rotate toward the front leg, then place your hands down.
2. Straighten the front leg briefly, return to the lunge, and step through to stand.
3. Alternate sides with controlled transitions and a pain-free range.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a slow, controlled, full-body demonstration of Complex Mobility Flow.
From standing, step the right foot back into a comfortable reverse lunge. Rotate
the chest toward the left front leg, then hinge and place both hands on the
floor near that foot. Briefly straighten the front leg without locking it,
bending the knee again to return to the lunge. Lift the hands and step through
to stand. Repeat the complete sequence with the other leg stepping back. Keep
the transitions smooth, the front foot grounded, and the range comfortable.
Do not turn this into a jump or add a push-up.

## 159 - Deep Recovery Flow

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Move slowly through a half-kneeling hip stretch, half split, and gentle rotation.
2. Pause for one relaxed breath in each position and keep the joints comfortable.
3. Reverse the sequence before changing sides.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a gentle, full-body recovery demonstration of Deep Recovery Flow. On
the right side, begin in a comfortable half-kneeling hip stretch with the right
foot forward and left knee supported on the floor. Pause for one relaxed breath.
Shift the hips back and extend the front leg into a comfortable half split;
pause for one relaxed breath. Return toward half-kneeling and make a gentle
upper-body rotation toward the front leg; pause for one relaxed breath. Reverse
the positions in order to return to the start, then switch legs and repeat.
Keep the spine long, transitions unhurried, and all joint ranges comfortable;
never force a stretch or bounce.

## 160 - Prone Posture Control Flow

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Lie face down with your forehead hovering and arms reaching overhead.
2. Lift your arms slightly, sweep them into a W shape, and draw the shoulder blades down.
3. Reach overhead again and lower with control while keeping the neck long.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a clear full-body floor demonstration of Prone Posture Control Flow. Lie
face down with legs extended, forehead hovering just above the floor, neck long,
and both arms reaching overhead in a comfortable Y position. Lift both arms
slightly, sweep them smoothly into a W beside the upper body, and draw the
shoulder blades down without shrugging. Sweep both arms back overhead and lower
them with control to the starting position. Keep the pelvis and legs relaxed on
the floor; do not arch the lower back or lift the head.

## 169 - Seated Direction-change Drill

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Sit tall on the floor with knees bent and brace the trunk lightly.
2. Perform two quick cross-body reaches, two heel taps, and one overhead reach.
3. Reverse the lead side each round while keeping the sequence precise.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a precise, full-body seated demonstration of Seated Direction-change
Drill. Sit tall on the floor with knees bent, feet visible, and trunk lightly
braced. In each round perform exactly two quick cross-body reaches, first
leading with one side and then the other; perform two alternating heel taps to
the floor; then perform one controlled overhead reach. Keep the torso tall and
the movements small and crisp. In the next round reverse which side leads the
first cross-body reach. Show the sequence clearly without adding punches,
extra taps, or a seated twist.

## 170 - Seated Core Bracing Matrix

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Sit tall on the floor with knees bent and lean back slightly without rounding.
2. Reach both arms forward, then diagonally to each side while resisting trunk rotation.
3. Return upright between rounds and stop if the lower back loses position.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a controlled, full-body demonstration of Seated Core Bracing Matrix. Sit
on the floor with knees bent and feet planted, establish a tall neutral spine,
then lean back only slightly without rounding the lower back. Reach both arms
straight forward together. Return to center and reach both arms diagonally to
one side while keeping the rib cage and pelvis facing forward; return to center
and repeat to the other side. Sit fully upright between rounds. Keep the range
small and steady; do not twist the trunk or let the lower back round.

## 172 - Squat Thrust to Push-up

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Squat down, place both hands on the floor, and step or jump into a strong plank.
2. Perform one controlled push-up, keeping the trunk aligned.
3. Return the feet toward the hands and stand without adding an unnecessary jump.

**Animation prompt:**

Create one controlled, full-body repetition of Squat Thrust to Push-up. From
standing, squat and place both palms on the floor. Step the feet back one at a
time into a strong high plank. Perform exactly one controlled push-up while
keeping shoulders, trunk, hips, and legs aligned. Step both feet forward toward
the hands, then stand upright without jumping. Keep both hands and feet visible
throughout; do not add a burpee jump or extra push-up.

## 173 - Cossack Squat Mobility Flow

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Take a wide stance and shift the hips toward one side as that knee bends.
2. Keep the opposite leg long and the chest lifted within a comfortable depth.
3. Press through the bent leg to transition smoothly to the other side.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a smooth, full-body demonstration of Cossack Squat Mobility Flow. Begin
in a wide, stable stance with toes comfortably turned out. Shift the hips toward
the right, bending the right knee while keeping the left leg long and the chest
lifted. Press through the bent right leg to travel smoothly through center and
shift toward the left, bending the left knee while the right leg stays long.
Repeat both directions at a comfortable depth. Keep each heel grounded when
possible, knees tracking with toes, and transitions controlled; do not bounce
or force the range.

## 174 - Inchworm Push-up Walkout

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Hinge forward, place your hands down, and walk them into a high plank.
2. Complete one controlled push-up while keeping the body aligned.
3. Walk the hands back, soften the knees as needed, and stand tall.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a clear, full-body demonstration of Inchworm Push-up Walkout. Start
standing with feet stable. Hinge at the hips, soften the knees as needed, and
place both hands on the floor. Walk the hands forward step by step until the
body reaches a strong high plank. Perform one controlled push-up while keeping
head, trunk, hips, and legs aligned. Walk the hands back toward the feet, bend
the knees as needed, and stand tall. Keep the feet in place during the hand
walk; do not add a jump or extra repetition.

## 175 - Ninety-ninety Rotation Recovery

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Sit in a ninety-ninety hip position with both sit bones comfortable on the floor.
2. Rotate the rib cage toward the front shin and reach the opposite arm upward.
3. Return slowly, complete both sides, and avoid forcing the hip range.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a gentle, full-body demonstration of Ninety-ninety Rotation Recovery.
Sit on the floor with both legs bent at approximately ninety degrees, one leg
in front and the other to the side, and both sit bones comfortable on the
floor. Rotate the rib cage toward the front shin while reaching the opposite
arm upward. Return slowly to the upright 90/90 seat. Switch the legs to the
opposite 90/90 arrangement and repeat the rotation and opposite-arm reach.
Keep the movement in a comfortable range, avoid forcing the hips, and do not
lift either sit bone aggressively.

## 176 - Thread-the-needle Recovery Flow

**Instructions from `exercises.py` (verbatim):**

1. Begin on hands and knees and slide one arm beneath the other across the floor.
2. Rotate gently through the upper back, then sweep that arm toward the ceiling.
3. Repeat with control on both sides while keeping the hips centered.

**Animation prompt:**

Create a gentle, full-body demonstration of Thread-the-needle Recovery Flow.
Begin on hands and knees with wrists under shoulders, knees under hips, and hips
centered. Keeping the hips steady, slide the right arm beneath the left arm
across the floor and gently rotate the upper back. Return the right arm through
center, then sweep it upward toward the ceiling with a comfortable chest
rotation. Return to hands and knees and repeat the same sequence with the left
arm. Keep both knees grounded and the neck relaxed; do not shift the hips
sideways or force the rotation.

## External generation and review steps

1. Use an original text-to-video or animation generator that can export an
   animated image. Submit one exercise prompt at a time, applying the shared
   visual requirements.
2. Generate a short clip with a clear complete repetition or sequence and a
   return to the opening pose where appropriate. Avoid stylized or copyrighted
   characters, logos, and reference footage.
3. Review the full clip frame-by-frame. Reject it if the sequence is out of
   order, a required side is absent, any limb is missing or distorted, the
   camera moves, or the movement differs from the verbatim instructions.
4. Export an animated GIF named for its exercise ID. For ID 157, first save to
   a separate staging filename; do not replace the existing `157.gif` without
   permission. Keep the existing `176.gif` untouched unless its source and
   usage rights are established and replacement is approved.
5. After review, place an approved GIF in `static\exercises\`, verify it has
   multiple valid frames, register only that verified file in
   `exercise_media.py`, then test its static URL and actual recommendation
   card.
